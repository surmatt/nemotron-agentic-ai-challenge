NAXOS, or NAXIA, a considerable town, and capital of the Isle of Naxos, opposite the Isle of Paros, with a castle and two archbishops' sees, the one Greek and the other Latin. The greater part of the inhabitants are Greeks. Long. 25. 51. E. Lat. 37. 8. N. Greeks. Long. 25. 51. E. Lat. 37. 8. N. NAYO, a small island in the Eastern Seas, near to the north coast of Celebes. Long. 124. 24. E. Lat. 1. 24. N. NAYRES are the nobility of the Malabar coast. We may with truth affirm that they are the oldest nobility in the world; for the most ancient writers mention them, and quote the law which permits the Nayre ladies to have several husbands, every one being allowed four.
