EXECUTION, in the law of Scotland. See Law Index. EXECUTION, in the French music, is used to denote the manner of singing, or of the performance of a song. " As to the manner of singing, called in France execu- tion, no nation may, with any probability dispute it with the French. If the French, by their commerce with the Italians have gained a bolder composition, the Italians have made their advantage of the French, in learning of them a more polite, moving, and exqui- site execution." St Evremond.
