EXEMPLAR, a model, or original, to be imitated, or copied. See Model. EXEMPLAR also denotes the idea, or image, conceived formed in the mind of the artist, whereby he con- sists his work. Such is the idea of Cedar, which a painter has in his mind when he goes to make a picture of Cedar. EXEMPLIFICATION of Letters Patent, denotes EXEMPLIFICATION of Letters Patent, denotes an exemplar, or copy of letters patent, made from the enrolment thereof, and sealed with the great seal of England. Such exemplifications are as effectual to be owed or pleaded, as the letters patent themselves.
